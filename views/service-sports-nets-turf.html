Technical Twine & Mesh Size Guide: Twisted vs Braided Nets

Selecting the right twine thickness and mesh size is vital for structural longevity, ball retention, and cost-effectiveness. We supply genuine Russea™ HDPE netting precision-manufactured to exact industry standards:

📌 Twisted Nets (1.5mm Thickness — 35mm & 40mm Mesh Sizes)

Available Sizes: Exclusively available in 35mm mesh and 40mm mesh sizes.
Primary Application: 1.5mm HDPE Twisted Nets are predominantly engineered and installed for Top Roof Coverings in box cricket arenas and cricket practice cages. Because top nets experience aerial ball contact rather than direct point-blank high-speed leather ball strikes, 1.5mm twisted nets offer significant weight reduction, superior wind aerodynamic flow, reduced sagging, and optimal project economy.

📌 Braided Nets (2.0mm & 2.5mm Thickness — 40mm, 45mm & 50mm Mesh Sizes)

Available Sizes: Available in 2mm and 2.5mm twine with 40mm, 45mm, and 50mm mesh sizes.
Primary Application: 2mm and 2.5mm HDPE Braided Nets provide heavy-duty multi-filament interlocking strength designed to endure non-stop high-velocity impacts (150+ km/h leather cricket balls, hard football strikes). They are universally recommended for all sides (side walls, bowler run-ups, batsman impact zones) and can also be used for top roof enclosures where maximum extreme durability is required.

Our 5-Stage Certified Installation Protocol

Every installation adheres to rigorous structural engineering and aesthetic standards:

Step 1: Site Survey & Layout 3D CAD Planning

We measure court dimensions, clearance heights, lighting angles, and spectator buffer zones.

Step 2: Structural Pole Fabrication & Civil Anchoring

Heavy-gauge MS/GI tubular framework is erected with reinforced concrete footings.

Step 3: Perimeter Wire Rope Suspension Grid

Multi-strand steel cables are cross-tensioned across the roof and perimeter walls to form a rigid netting cage.

Step 4: Russea™ Sports Net Installation

Heavy 2mm/3mm braided Russea™ netting is laced to the steel grid, with reinforced drop nets behind batting wickets.

Step 5: Sub-Base Preparation & Turf Laying

Aggregate base compaction, shock-pad laying, artificial turf joint taping, and silica sand/SBR rubber infill spreading.

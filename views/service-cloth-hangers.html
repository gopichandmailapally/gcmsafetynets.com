Ceiling-Mounted Stainless Steel Cloth Drying Hangers

In modern high-rise apartments, balcony floor space is precious. Clunky floor-standing drying racks clutter your outdoor area, blow over in high winds, and block natural sunlight. Ceiling cloth drying hangers provide an elegant, space-saving solution that utilizes ceiling airspace while keeping your balcony floor 100% free.

GCM Safety Nets manufactures and installs premium Heavy-Duty Stainless Steel (SS) Ceiling Cloth Drying Hangers. Built with 100% rust-proof SS 304 stainless steel pipes, individual pulley mechanisms, and high-strength braided nylon cordage, each pipe can easily hold 5 to 8 kg of wet laundry.

Key Features of GCM Stainless Steel Ceiling Hangers

  • 100% Rust-Proof SS 304 Pipes: High-grade stainless steel pipes that will never rust, warp, or leave orange iron stains on your white clothes.
  • Independent Pulley Lowering System: Each pipe lowers and raises individually to your preferred height, allowing effortless hanging for children and seniors without straining.
  • Dual-Wheel Nylon Pulley Bearings: Ultra-smooth friction-free pulley wheels that make lifting heavy, wet bedsheets and blankets effortless.
  • High-Strength Braided Nylon Ropes: Heavy-gauge UV-treated nylon cords with locking wall cleats that securely hold each pipe in place.
  • Custom Pipe Lengths (4ft to 8ft): Available in 4, 5, 6, and 8-pipe configurations tailored to fit any balcony, utility corridor, or laundry room ceiling.

Our 5-Stage Certified Installation Protocol

Every installation adheres to rigorous structural engineering and aesthetic standards:

Step 1: Ceiling Measurement & Layout Audit

We inspect ceiling height, ceiling fans, light fixtures, and sliding door clearances.

Step 2: Ceiling Bracket Mounting

Heavy-duty dual-pulley ceiling brackets are anchored securely into the concrete slab.

Step 3: Wall Cleat & Lock Installation

A multi-hook metal locking cleat is mounted on the adjacent wall at comfortable operating height.

Step 4: Stainless Steel Pipe & Rope Threading

High-tenacity nylon cords are threaded through the pulley wheels and attached to the SS 304 pipes.

Step 5: Full Weight Testing & Operational Demo

Our technician tests each pipe with simulated laundry load and demonstrates smooth raising and lowering.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much weight can each ceiling drying pipe hold?

Each GCM SS 304 pipe comfortably supports 5 kg to 8 kg of wet laundry, making it ideal for heavy bedsheets, jeans, and winter blankets.

What ceiling height is required for installation?

Ceiling hangers can be installed on any concrete ceiling from 8 feet up to 14 feet in height with custom rope lengths.

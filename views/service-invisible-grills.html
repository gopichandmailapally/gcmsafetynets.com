Architectural Marine-Grade Stainless Steel Invisible Grills

Modern luxury apartments, villas, and commercial penthouses demand unobstructed panoramic views without compromising on structural fall safety. Traditional bulky mild steel (MS) grilles look like prison bars, block emergency escape routes, rust over time, and frequently violate gated society facade guidelines.

GCM Safety Nets installs high-tensile AISI 316 Marine-Grade Stainless Steel Invisible Grills. Constructed with 2.5mm to 3.0mm multi-strand SS 316 wire ropes sheathed in transparent anti-UV nylon casing, our invisible grills withstand over 400+ kg tensile breaking loads while remaining virtually invisible from a distance of 15 feet.

Our 5-Stage Certified Installation Protocol

Every installation adheres to rigorous structural engineering and aesthetic standards:

Step 1: Laser Perimeter Survey

We measure the window or balcony opening and determine vertical or horizontal cable orientation.

Step 2: Heavy Aluminium H-Track Channel Mounting

Solid extruded aluminium tracks with nylon locking grooves are anchored into concrete using SS expansion fasteners.

Step 3: SS 316 Wire Rope Threading

Continuous multi-strand SS 316 cable is woven through the aluminium tracks at 2-inch or 3-inch gap intervals.

Step 4: Hydraulic Tension Calibration

The cables are tensioned using calibrated mechanical levers to ensure maximum tautness with zero hand-spreading slack.

Step 5: Cover Capping & Lock Finishing

Aesthetic aluminium snap-on cover caps conceal all fastener bolts, providing a sleek, modern architectural finish.

GCM Safety Nets manufactures and installs custom Industrial-Grade Russea™ 100% Virgin HDPE Netting. Our 5-Stage Certified Installation Protocol

Every installation adheres to rigorous structural engineering and aesthetic standards:

Step 1: Shaft Void & Duct Dimension Mapping

We survey central ventilation duct spans, pipe conduits, and structural anchor beams.

Step 2: Primary Steel Cable Grid Erection

Galvanized and SS 316 guide wires are cross-strung across the duct void every 15 to 20 feet.

Step 3: Heavy Anchor Fastening

Mechanical anchor bolts are fixed along RCC slab perimeters to support high structural wind loads.

Step 4: Russea™ Industrial Net Interlocking

The Russea™ virgin HDPE netting is laced and clamped securely to the guide cable grid.

Step 5: Tension & Access Hatch Integration

We verify uniform surface tension and install zippered access flaps for AC and plumbing servicing.

🔬 MATERIAL SCIENCE & POLYMER ENGINEERING

The Engineering Superiority of Russea™ 100% Virgin HDPE Monofilament Twines

Not all synthetic netting materials are created equal. Low-cost unbranded nets sold in the open market frequently utilize recycled polymer scraps that lack molecular cohesion and contain volatile fillers. Under continuous outdoor sunlight, solar UV radiation breaks the molecular polymer bonds, causing the nets to become brittle, fade, and snap under minimal load.

GCM Safety Nets exclusively deploys certified Russea™ High-Density Polyethylene (HDPE) netting extruded from 100% prime virgin raw polymers. Infused with UV 50+ SunShield hindered amine light stabilizers (HALS), Russea™ netting is hydrophobic (0% water absorption), highly chemical resistant, and engineered to endure over 5 years of harsh outdoor climatic exposure.

Life-Saving Child & Pet Fall Protection Systems

Children are naturally curious explorers, and domestic pets (especially cats and dogs) are agile jumpers who can easily slip through railing gaps, climb onto furniture near parapets, or lose their balance on high-rise balconies, staircases, and window ledges. In multi-storey apartments, an accidental slip can be catastrophic.

GCM Safety Nets installs heavy-duty Russea™ brand Child & Pet Safety Nets designed specifically to absorb massive dynamic impact loads of up to 150 kg to 250 kg. Our systems transform dangerous balconies, open terrace voids, internal stairwells, and French windows into 100% secure, child-proof play zones.

Certified Heavy-Duty Russea™ Construction

  • 100% Virgin High-Tenacity Braided HDPE: Multi-ply braided twine engineered with extreme tensile elongation to cushion sudden falls without rebounding injuries.
  • Cat-Proof Claws & Bite Resistance: High-density polymer filaments resist scratching, chewing, and clawing from energetic indoor cats and dogs.
  • Safe Knotless/Heat-Set Weave: Smooth surface prevents friction burns or pinched fingers for crawling toddlers and small children.
  • Marine Grade SS 304 Reinforcement: Heavy stainless steel perimeter cables anchored deeply into structural concrete columns and slabs.
  • Full 5-Year Replacement Guarantee: Guaranteed against material fatigue, sagging, or environmental degradation.

Our 5-Stage Certified Installation Protocol

Every installation adheres to rigorous structural engineering and aesthetic standards:

Step 1: Fall Risk & Gap Assessment

We inspect railing heights, vertical bar spacing, furniture positioning, and staircase open voids.

Step 2: High-Density Anchor Drilling

Heavy-duty SS 304 hooks are anchored every 6 to 8 inches to ensure zero slack under dynamic weight.

Step 3: Reinforced Steel Cable Webbing

A multi-strand 4mm stainless steel perimeter tension wire is locked across the entire perimeter.

Step 4: Russea™ Braided Net Interlocking

The high-strength Russea™ netting is mechanically interlocked to the steel frame, eliminating any toe-holds.

Step 5: Manual Impact & Stress Testing

Our installers apply full-body tension tests to guarantee 100% fail-safe security before project handover.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can a child cut or untie the safety nets?

No. Russea™ nets are installed using mechanical tamper-proof steel crimps and high-tenacity braided HDPE that cannot be untied or easily cut by children.

Is this netting safe for cats and dogs?

Yes. The 25mm to 40mm mesh spacing prevents cats from slipping through or getting their heads stuck, while the braided polymer resists claws and chewing.

Can these nets be installed on open stairwells and duct voids?

Yes, we install custom child safety netting across multi-storey staircase central shafts, open ducts, and duplex balconies.

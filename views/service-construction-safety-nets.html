IS 5175 Compliant Construction Fall Arrest & Debris Containment Nets

High-rise building construction, civil infrastructure projects, and commercial scaffolding sites carry severe occupational safety hazards. Falling tools, concrete debris, and worker fall risks can result in tragic injuries, heavy regulatory fines, and construction halts under Indian Labor and Factory Acts.

GCM Safety Nets is India’s certified industrial supplier of IS 5175 compliant construction safety nets and scaffolding perimeter catch nets. Manufactured using 100% genuine Russea™ high-tenacity polypropylene and virgin HDPE, our dual-layer netting systems combine high-impact fall arrest nets with fine debris containment overlays.

Our 5-Stage Certified Installation Protocol

Every installation adheres to rigorous structural engineering and aesthetic standards:

Step 1: Site Risk & Scaffolding Geometry Audit

We inspect building elevation, cantilever outriggers, floor drops, and perimeter scaffolding anchors.

Step 2: Outrigger & Structural Bracket Rigging

Heavy structural steel outrigger arms are fixed along exterior slab edges to extend the catch perimeter.

Step 3: Border Tie-Rope Anchor Fixing

The 12mm heavy-duty perimeter rope is tied and shackled securely to structural columns and scaffolding pipes.

Step 4: Dual-Layer Net Interlocking

The Russea™ primary fall arrest net and secondary fine debris containment mesh are lashed with heavy-gauge tie cords.

Step 5: Load Testing & Safety Compliance Signoff

Visual inspection and tension verification compliant with site safety audit requirements.

On high-rise commercial and residential construction projects across India, fall hazards represent the leading cause of industrial workplace injuries. Municipal building safety authorities strictly mandate the deployment of IS 5175 certified personnel fall arrest systems and vertical perimeter scaffolding containment nets.

GCM Safety Nets manufactures and erects heavy-duty dual-layer construction safety nets engineered from authentic Russea™ high-tenacity braided HDPE and polypropylene ropes. Designed to absorb dynamic kinetic fall energy exceeding 1,200 Joules, our safety nets protect construction workers, site engineers, and pedestrians passing at ground level.

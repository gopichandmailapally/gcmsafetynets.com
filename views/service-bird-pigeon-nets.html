Our 5-Stage Certified Installation Protocol

Every installation adheres to rigorous structural engineering and aesthetic standards:

Step 1: Inspection & Roosting Area Audit

Technicians inspect bird nesting hotspots, duct openings, and AC ledges, noting all structural boundaries.

Step 2: Sanitization & Dropping Clearance

Prior to netting installation, our team helps clear existing bird droppings to restore hygienic conditions.

Step 3: SS 304 Perimeter Frame Mounting

Stainless steel anchor pins are drilled at close intervals around the entire opening perimeter.

Step 4: Tension Cable Stringing

A heavy stainless steel perimeter wire rope is tensioned tightly using barrel strainers to prevent sagging.

Step 5: Russea™ Net Weaving & Interlocking

The Russea™ virgin HDPE netting is fastened knot-by-knot to the steel border, creating an impenetrable barrier.

Comprehensive Pigeon Health Hazard Analysis & Engineered Netting Solutions

Urban pigeon infestation is one of the fastest-growing environmental health hazards across Indian metropolitan centers. Pigeons roosting on apartment balconies, window AC ledges, internal ventilation ducts, and commercial atriums generate large quantities of droppings and nesting debris. When dried bird guano is disturbed by air conditioner fans or natural wind currents, it releases microscopic fungal spores into indoor living spaces, triggering severe respiratory conditions including Histoplasmosis, Cryptococcosis, and Hypersensitivity Pneumonitis (Bird Fancier's Lung).

In addition to bio-health hazards, pigeon droppings contain concentrated uric acid (pH 3.0 to 4.5) that chemically corrodes exterior building plaster, peels paint, and damages expensive copper cooling coils in split air conditioner outdoor units. GCM Safety Nets provides the permanent, humane physical barrier solution using 100% genuine Russea™ UV 50+ stabilized High-Density Polyethylene (HDPE) netting that permanently prevents birds from landing while preserving 90%+ natural daylight and fresh air circulation.

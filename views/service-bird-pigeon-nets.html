100% Humane & Permanent Anti-Bird & Pigeon Control

Urban pigeons and feral birds pose substantial health, hygienic, and property hazards. Pigeon droppings contain highly corrosive uric acid that damages building facades, air conditioning units, and painted surfaces. More critically, dried droppings release pathogenic fungal spores responsible for severe respiratory infections including Histoplasmosis, Cryptococcosis, and Psittacosis.

GCM Safety Nets installs genuine Russea™ brand 100% virgin High-Density Polyethylene (HDPE) anti-bird netting. Unlike harmful chemical deterrents, electric shocks, or traps, our precision micro-mesh physically blocks birds from perching, roosting, or nesting without causing them any physical harm.

Engineered with Certified Russea™ High-Tenacity Monofilament

Our anti-pigeon netting systems are designed specifically for high-rise residential balconies, duct areas, utility corridors, AC compressor ledges, and industrial warehouse rafters:

  • Optimal 25mm to 30mm Mesh Size: Specifically calibrated to exclude small pigeons, sparrows, and crows while maintaining 100% unimpeded airflow and clear external visibility.
  • 100% Russea™ Virgin Polymer: Heat-set knotted monofilament that will never stretch, sag, or unravel when subjected to high-velocity storm winds.
  • UV 300+ KLY Weatherization: Treated with European UV chemical stabilizers to prevent sun degradation, fading, or cracking under 45°C+ summer heat.
  • Non-Conductive & Chemically Inert: Zero water absorption, zero rotting in torrential rains, and total resistance to fungal growth.
  • Zero Building Facade Damage: Fixed using sleek stainless steel anchors and border tension cables that preserve exterior paint and plaster.

Our 5-Stage Certified Installation Protocol

Every installation adheres to rigorous structural engineering and aesthetic standards:

Step 1: Inspection & Roosting Area Audit

Technicians inspect bird nesting hotspots, duct openings, and AC ledges, noting all structural boundaries.

Step 2: Sanitization & Dropping Clearance

Prior to netting installation, our team helps clear existing bird droppings to restore hygienic conditions.

Step 3: SS 304 Perimeter Frame Mounting

Stainless steel anchor pins are drilled at close intervals around the entire opening perimeter.

Step 4: Tension Cable Stringing

A heavy stainless steel perimeter wire rope is tensioned tightly using barrel strainers to prevent sagging.

Step 5: Russea™ Net Weaving & Interlocking

The Russea™ virgin HDPE netting is fastened knot-by-knot to the steel border, creating an impenetrable barrier.
